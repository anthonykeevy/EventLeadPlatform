## 2. Product Positioning

EventLead is not just an event lead form builder.

Position it as:

> A customer engagement form platform for marketing, events and operations teams who need to create branded forms, capture useful customer data, and manage the process from build to approval to follow-up.

The landing page should support both product lenses:

### Operational Lens

For hands-on users who build and publish forms:

- Build branded forms without developer help.
- Capture event leads, registrations, surveys, inquiries and feedback.
- Use public links, website embeds, QR codes and kiosk-style capture.
- Reduce manual rework and spreadsheet handoffs.

### Platform Lens

For managers and enterprise buyers:

- See forms, campaigns and customer data across teams or business units.
- Add approval and governance before forms go live.
- Support company workspaces, roles, audit trails and sharing.
- Turn form creation into a managed workflow, not an ad hoc tool sprawl.

AEO means making the page easy for AI answer engines to understand and quote.

The page should answer direct questions in plain text:

Use real questions:

**What is EventLead?**  
EventLead is a customer engagement form platform for creating branded forms, capturing customer data, and managing the workflow around approvals, sharing and follow-up.

**Who is EventLead for?**  
EventLead is for marketing teams, event teams, agencies and organisations that need to collect customer information through forms and manage what happens before and after submission.

You can use EventLead for event lead capture, registrations, RSVPs, customer feedback, surveys, product inquiries, kiosk capture and campaign forms.

Generic form builders focus on collecting answers. EventLead focuses on the broader customer engagement workflow, including branding, approvals, team roles, sharing, event context and follow-up.

For the test system, use careful wording such as: EventLead is currently in early testing. Some capabilities are available now, and others are being validated with customers before production release.

## 9. Messaging Do / Don't

### Do

- Say "customer engagement forms" more than "forms".
- Explain outcomes: faster launch, cleaner capture, better follow-up, more visibility.
- Acknowledge events as the wedge but not the whole category.
- Keep the tone practical and founder-led.
- Use plain English.

### Don't

- Lead with "multi-tenant SaaS", "RBAC", "FastAPI", "AI-powered" or developer terms.
- Overpromise production readiness.
- Claim enterprise compliance unless it is actually implemented.
- Position as just another form builder.
- Make the visitor work to understand what the product does.

## 12. Product Decisions And Follow-Up Tasks

### Decisions

1. **Product name:** unresolved. Anthony has a separate naming project/output to review before finalising whether the public name is `EventLead`, `Signal Platforms EventLead`, or another name. For implementation, use `EventLead` as a placeholder brand string and keep it easy to change.
2. **Launch state:** use **Beta** language.
3. **Primary CTA path:** route `Create an account` to the current sign-up path.
4. **Pricing:** while in Beta, the platform is free to use.
5. **Beta migration offer:** if someone starts using the Beta platform, Signal Platforms may offer to migrate them to production for free if they let us know. Do **not** publish this as a promise until risks are reviewed.
6. **Screenshots:** no current screenshots are safe/ready for public use. Create realistic examples using the actual form builder instead of showing developer/test data.

### Required Follow-Up Tasks

#### Task 1 — Prepare Privacy Policy And Terms Links

**Why:** The landing page needs privacy and terms links before it is used with external prospects.

**Deliverable:**

- Public `/privacy` page or linked privacy policy.
- Public `/terms` page or linked terms of use.
- Footer links added to the landing page.
- Beta wording checked so it does not overpromise reliability, support, compliance, production migration or data handling.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Footer includes working Privacy and Terms links.
- Beta terms explain that the system is in testing.
- No legal/compliance claims are made unless verified.
- Anthony reviews before publishing externally.

#### Task 2 — Create Safe Example Forms In The Form Builder

**Why:** The landing page needs credible visuals and examples, but no current screenshots are ready.

**Deliverable:** Build sample forms using the real form builder for the major use cases:

- Event lead capture
- Test-drive / demo request
- Registration / RSVP
- Customer feedback / NPS
- Product inquiry
- Kiosk / reception capture
- Agency / client campaign form

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Examples use realistic but fake data.
- Examples do not expose test users, internal company names, secrets, or unfinished admin screens.
- At least one example demonstrates brand styling/custom background.
- At least one example demonstrates event or campaign context.
- Screenshots or embedded previews are suitable for the landing page.

#### Task 3 — Review Beta Free-Use And Migration Offer Risk

**Why:** Free Beta access can reduce friction, but a production-migration promise may create operational, data, support and legal obligations.

**Questions to answer before publishing the offer:**

- What data can Beta users safely enter?
- Will Beta data be backed up?
- What exactly does "migrate to production for free" include?
- Are there limits on number of forms, submissions, files or users?
- What happens if Beta data structure changes before production?
- What support level is offered during Beta?
- Does the migration offer apply to all users or only selected design partners?

**Recommended interim wording:**

> EventLead is currently free to use during Beta. If you start using it seriously, let us know so we can work with you on the safest path to production when the platform is ready.

Avoid promising automatic or unlimited free migration until the above risks are resolved.
