# Public Event Guidelines Policy

**EventLead Platform - Public Event Quality Standards**

---

## Purpose

To ensure a high-quality experience for all users on the EventLead Platform, we review all events before they become publicly visible. This quality check helps maintain professional standards and prevents inappropriate content from appearing in public event searches.

---

## What We Review

Our review process checks for:

### ✅ **Content Quality**
- Appropriate language (no profanity, offensive terms, or hate speech)
- Accurate and complete event information
- Clear and professional event descriptions
- Proper categorization and tagging

### ✅ **Data Completeness**
- Event name and description
- Date and time information
- Location details (for physical/hybrid events)
- Event type and industry classification

### ✅ **Professional Standards**
- No spam or misleading information
- No duplicate events
- Respectful and appropriate content
- Compliance with platform terms of service

---

## Review Process

1. **Submission**: When you mark an event as "Public" and enable "Share with Platform", it is submitted for review
2. **Review Time**: Our team typically reviews events within 24-48 hours
3. **Approval**: If approved, your event will be visible to all users on the platform when you publish it
4. **Rejection**: If rejected, you'll receive feedback on what needs to be addressed

---

## What Happens After Review

### ✅ **Approved Events**
- Your event is approved for public visibility
- Once you publish the event (EventStatus = PUBLISHED), it will appear in public event searches
- Other companies on the platform can discover and link to your event

### ❌ **Rejected Events**
- Your event cannot be made public on the platform
- **However**, your event remains fully functional for your company and linked organizations
- You can still use the event internally for lead capture and management
- You can edit the event and resubmit for review after addressing the feedback

---

## Common Rejection Reasons

Events may be rejected if they contain:

- **Offensive Language**: Profanity, hate speech, or inappropriate content
- **Incomplete Information**: Missing required fields (description, location, etc.)
- **Misleading Information**: False or deceptive event details
- **Spam Content**: Duplicate or promotional spam
- **Inappropriate Content**: Content that violates platform terms of service

---

## Resubmission Process

If your event is rejected:

1. **Review Feedback**: Read the rejection feedback provided by our review team
2. **Edit Event**: Make necessary changes to address the feedback
3. **Resubmit**: Click "Resubmit for Review" to send your updated event for review again
4. **Review Again**: Our team will review your updated event (typically within 24-48 hours)

---

## Event Visibility Options

You have control over how your event is shared:

### **Private Event**
- Visible only to your company
- No review required
- Full functionality for internal use

### **Public Event (Company Network Only)**
- Visible to your company and linked organizations
- No review required
- Not visible in platform-wide public search
- Useful for events shared with partners or suppliers

### **Public Event (Platform-Wide)**
- Visible to your company and linked organizations
- **AND** visible in platform-wide public search
- **Requires admin review** for quality assurance
- Other companies can discover and link to your event
